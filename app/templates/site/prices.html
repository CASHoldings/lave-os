Collection and delivery

How quickly will I get my clothes back?

We collect and deliver Monday to Saturday, 6am to 10pm. Most cleaning, wash & fold and pressing comes back from {{ terms.turnaround }} hours after we collect: book a 6–7am collection on Monday and we can return it from 7am on Wednesday. Specialist pieces, alterations, repairs, curtains and rugs take longer; we'll confirm a date once we've seen them. If your return would fall on a Sunday or bank holiday, it moves to the next day we're open.

Is collection and delivery free?

Yes, in a 3-hour Saver window on orders from {{ terms.threshold }}. Under {{ terms.threshold }} it's {{ terms.under }}. A one-hour window is {{ terms.hour }}. Members always get Saver free; one-hour is {{ terms.essence_hour }} for Essence and included for Elevate and Éclat. One charge covers both collection and return.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. For a home collection, your items need to come to {{ terms.home_minimum }} or more; members dropping off at a LAVE atelier need {{ terms.dropoff_minimum }}. Specialist pieces count at their "from" price, and curated packs can always be booked on their own. If you book without a list and your items come to less once we've counted them, we charge the minimum.

Where do you collect from?

We currently collect and deliver across SW11, Nine Elms and Battersea Power Station. We're expanding across south-west London, so join the waitlist if we're not with you yet.

Do you work on Sundays?

We don't collect or deliver on Sundays. Members can drop items off at a LAVE atelier when it's open, including Sundays, and we'll treat them as collected on Monday morning.

Prices include cleaning and finishing. LAVE isn't VAT-registered, so no VAT is added.
