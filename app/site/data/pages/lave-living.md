## Support · What's on offer

- **Someone to talk to.** A confidential helpline, with counselling by phone or video when you need more support.
- **Help at home.** Practical guidance on budgets, debt and everyday legal questions, from tenancy to moving home.
- **Support at work.** Advice on pressure at work, returning to work and coping with change.
- **Feel better, step by step.** Four-week plans for sleeping better, eating well and managing stress.
- **Everything in one app.** Mood tracking, mini health checks, guided breathing, guides and videos, wherever you are.

## Membership · With your membership

- **Essence:** wellbeing resources, guides and the app.
- **Elevate:** full LAVE Living support, including the helpline and counselling.
- **Éclat:** full LAVE Living support for your household.

## Privacy · Private, always

![](https://lavelondon.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/LAVE_Fabricare_Service_Bedroom.webp)

Your conversations are with our partner's qualified team, not with LAVE, and stay between you and them.

*LAVE Living is provided by a specialist wellbeing partner, not by LAVE, and isn't medical advice. Counselling is offered after an initial assessment. If you're in crisis or at risk, call 999, or Samaritans free on 116 123.*
