## Cookies we use

| Name | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lave_client | Keeps you signed in to your account | 14 days |
| lave_list | Remembers the pieces on your list | 60 days |
| lave_basket | Remembers your Apothecary basket | 30 days |
| lave_staff | Keeps LAVE team members signed in to our admin | 12 hours |
| \_\_stripe_mid, \_\_stripe_sid | Set by Stripe on payment pages to prevent fraud | 1 year; 30 minutes |

## Other technology

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** checks that people signing up are human. It's used only for security.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.
- Our booking page keeps a few website settings in your browser during your visit, so it loads faster. They're cleared when you close the tab.

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cookies above, you won't be able to sign in, keep a list or book.
