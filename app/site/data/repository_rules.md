## 1. Pricing rules

- 1. Prices match American Dry Cleaning Company's Standard list (captured July 2026). Items American doesn't price: market reference × B8. No discounts.
- 2. Staples (shirts, tops, trousers, socks, underwear) are sold only in packs of B6 or more — single-type packs or mixed packs. No single staples. Pressing-only is sold singly.
- 3. SoFresh processes at 50% of its counter price; that is LAVE's cost per item.
- 4. Non-members: free collection and delivery from £45. Members: collection included.
- 5. LAVE is not VAT-registered and charges no VAT until it registers (at £90,000 turnover).
- 6. Quote only, never bundled: bridal and occasion wear, leather, suede, wax and fur, designer and luxury down coats, rugs, traditional garments, evening and beaded dresses, and major alterations (£40+). Quoted after inspection. Premier finish is on request only, not advertised.
- 7. Memberships: Essence £29, Elevate £59, Éclat £129 a month (Membership tab).
- 8. No item may lose money. Where neither American nor the market prices an item, price = SoFresh cost ÷ (1 − B10).

Key inputs: LAVE price = 1× American Standard · SoFresh paid 0.5 of counter price · minimum pack 5 items · free collection & delivery from £45 (non-members) · market uplift ×1.67 · press-only = 67% of cleaning price · minimum margin for cost-based prices 65%.

## 2. How items are sold

- **Packs only** — everyday staples (shirts, tops, trousers, jeans, shorts, socks, underwear). Never sold singly; minimum 5, mixable.
- **Single or pack** — can be bought one at a time or included in a mixed pack.
- **Single** — sold one at a time only (pressing-only, alterations, repairs, wash & fold bags, bed sets, shoes/bags, leather-trim items).
- **Quote** — priced after inspection, never bundled (bridal, leather/suede/fur, designer & down coats, traditional wear, evening/beaded dresses, rugs, major alterations). Price shown is the guide price.
- **Per m² / Per kg** — curtains, blinds, ironing, extra service wash.
- **Premier finish** — on request only, never advertised.

The "Service" column is derived from each item's category (dry clean, wash & press, laundry, specialist, etc.) and should be checked before it is used customer-facing.
